# Changes made by crocwork

This repository is a modified version of https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)).
The changes are made by https://github.com/CROC101/software-empire/blob/main/apps/toolboxes/tools/craft/rebrand.mjs:

- the app is named crocwork Word; ArtCraft, WordCraft and the upstream links are replaced in what users read
- the ArtCraft marks (docs/brand) and the app icons are replaced by crocwork artwork (icon glyph from Lucide, ISC)
- the Windows installer has its own UpgradeCode and publisher, and updates come from CROC101/crocwork-word
- the interface translations are a renamed copy of storytold/craft-i18n in craft-i18n/
- the build is .github/workflows/crocwork-release.yml (web and Windows, unsigned)

Copyright and licence notices are unchanged.
